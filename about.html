Ch. 01 — A short note Hey.
I'm Dennis.

I started this because I love IT customer support.

What we are: a small team — me, two techs, and a scheduling coordinator — who set up small businesses the way we'd set up our own. Real phone numbers. Plain-English invoices. No ticket portals you have to log into to complain that your Wi-Fi is down.

What we aren't: an MSP. We don't want 400 clients. We want forty clients we actually know the names of — the shift managers, the POS that's always rebooting, the printer the staff has named.

We're based in Ennis, TX and serve the full Dallas/Ft. Worth metro. Same-day on-site for emergencies. Remote-first for everything else. Text the number, get a human within the hour.

If that sounds like your kind of IT, text me. If not, no hard feelings — there's someone else out there for you and they probably have a ticket portal.
