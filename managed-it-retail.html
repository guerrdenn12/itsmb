The holiday-rush calculation nobody does.

Most retail IT looks fine in March. By Thanksgiving weekend, the network it was sized for is taking 4x the traffic, the POS is sharing bandwidth with 200 holiday-shopper phones, and the payment terminal that worked all year throws errors right when the line hits the door.

The fix isn't bigger hardware on December 1st. It's planning ahead — separated POS/guest networks, payment-terminal cellular failover, capacity sized for actual peak. We do the math in October so your Black Friday isn't a horror story.

Multi-location that actually syncs.

If you've got two locations on Shopify POS and a third opening in March, you don't need an enterprise warehouse system. You need stable site-to-site connectivity, central inventory, gift cards that pool across stores, and per-location reporting that runs Sunday morning so you can plan Monday.

Retail IT FAQ

What POS systems do you support for retail?

Square for Retail, Lightspeed Retail, Shopify POS, Clover, Vend/Lightspeed Vend, Heartland Retail. We focus on the network, devices, and integration around the POS — not the POS software itself.

Can you handle multi-location inventory?

Yes. Site-to-site VPN if needed, central inventory in your POS, per-location reporting, gift card pooling. We've set up 2-, 3-, and 5-location retail brands across DFW.

How do you handle the holiday rush?

Capacity planning ahead of the season — bandwidth, POS device count, payment-terminal failover, guest Wi-Fi sized for traffic. Plus on-call coverage for Black Friday, Small Business Saturday, and the December weekends.

What about online + in-store inventory sync?

Shopify, BigCommerce, WooCommerce, Square Online — most modern POS platforms have native sync, but the integration breaks more than vendors admit. We monitor it and catch the disconnects fast.

Do you handle the office side too — bookkeeping, payroll?

Yes. QuickBooks for bookkeeping, ADP/Gusto for payroll, WorkTrack for staff scheduling and clock-in (included with managed IT). All integrated.
