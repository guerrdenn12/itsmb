Pricing FAQ

Are there any contracts?

No long-term contracts. Setup is one-time. Monthly support is optional and you can cancel any time.

How fast can you have us set up?

Most clients go live in under a week. The 15-minute discovery call happens day 1, build happens day 2-5, and you're operational by the weekend.

Do you serve businesses outside Dallas/Ft. Worth?

We're based in Ennis, TX and serve the full DFW metro on-site. Remote support is available beyond DFW for select clients.

What if your service isn't a fit?

If we're not a fit after 30 days, we'll help you transition to someone who is. No penalty, no drama.

What's included in Setup Only?

WorkTrack configured for your team, employee PINs and GPS zones mapped, up to 5 devices set up, 1 hour of live team training, and 30 days of email support.
