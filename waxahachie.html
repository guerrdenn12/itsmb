What Waxahachie small businesses usually need.

Waxahachie's a different mix than Ennis. The historic square has more retail, more boutiques, more bed-and-breakfast operators. That changes our setup defaults. Email encryption gets enabled by default for legal and medical. Guest Wi-Fi gets aggressively isolated for retail. Backup verification is non-negotiable for anyone storing patient or client records.
