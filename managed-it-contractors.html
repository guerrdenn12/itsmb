IT for crews scattered across DFW.

You've got an office in Burleson, a foreman in Mansfield, and crews working from Garland to Mesquite. Managing payroll on paper isn't working anymore. Here's the IT side: GPS crew clock-in, mobile dispatch, payroll exports — set up once, runs every Friday.

The problem with paper time-tracking.

Most contractor offices we walk into have a stack of paper time slips on a clipboard, a foreman texting hours every Friday morning, and a bookkeeper rebuilding payroll from incomplete data. The math hurts: a $40/hr crew, 6-person team, 30 minutes of slop per day = $300/week of payroll noise. That's $15k/year per crew, lost.

The fix is GPS-based clock-in via WorkTrack — included with managed IT. Crew taps to clock in, geofence verifies they're actually on site, payroll export drops into QuickBooks Friday morning ready to run. Foreman sees a live dashboard of who's where instead of texting around.

The office side nobody talks about.

Beyond crew tracking, contractor offices have the same IT needs as any small office: email, file shares, secure backups, a phone, a printer that prints. We handle that too. The bookkeeper has access to QuickBooks. The owner can pull a job-cost report. The shared inbox doesn't lose customer emails.

Contractor IT FAQ

What kinds of contractors do you work with?

HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, roofing, fencing, concrete, framing, drywall, painting, cleaning, tree service. Anyone running 5+ field staff and needing accurate clock-in, payroll, and dispatch.

Do you handle GPS-based crew clock-in?

Yes — that's what WorkTrack is built for. Geofenced clock-in, automatic clock-out detection, payroll exports to QuickBooks/ADP/Gusto. Included with managed IT.

Can you support multiple crews on different job sites?

Yes. WorkTrack handles unlimited geofences, foreman dashboards show live crew status, and payroll exports break down by job for cost-coding. We've set up companies with 3-foreman / 25-crew operations across DFW.

What about the office side — bookkeeping, dispatch software?

We integrate with QuickBooks, ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro, and most field-service management tools. Office network, secure backups, email, file shares, multi-user printer that works.

Can you set up rugged tablets or phones for field crews?

Yes. We spec, image, and deploy ruggedized iPads/tablets for field crews, configure them with MDM (Mosyle, Microsoft Intune), enable remote wipe for lost devices, and lock them to the apps you actually want them using.
